# Roadster Rush

A Subway Surfers-style endless runner: fly the new Roadster through Manhattan traffic made up of Teslas and Waymos, twist over construction walls, and stay ahead of the patrol Cybertruck.

Play: https://ngold711.github.io/roadster-rush/

## Controls

| Move | Phone | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Change lanes | Swipe left / right | ← → or A / D |
| Thruster jump | Swipe up | ↑ or Space |
| Twist (flies higher) | Swipe up again in the air | ↑ again in the air |
| Drop fast | Swipe down in the air | ↓ |
| Pause / sound | II / SND buttons | P / M |

The whole game is the single `index.html` file.
